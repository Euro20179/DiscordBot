choose

params: *<from> (sep with |) (-picks <amount>) (-sep <sep by>)

Description: chooses -picks amount of <from>, defaults to 1 pick

Aliases: None

Added: 5/6/2020



coin

params: [h/t/flips]

Description: flips a coin, if bet is specified it'll say if you won or lost
giving heads/tails will indicate you want to bet
giving a number for [] will flip that many times

Aliases: None

Added: 1/18/2020



rand

params: <low> <high> [round] (--even) (--odd)

Description: picks a random number from <low> to <high>
if either is a decimal it'll give a decimal rounded to [round] defaults to no round
if --odd or --even it'll be an odd or even number

Aliases: None

Added: 1/18/2020
